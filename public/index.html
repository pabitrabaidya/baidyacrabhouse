Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our crabs, delivery, and guarantees

How can I order live Sundarban mud crabs online in Kolkata?

You can order directly from Baidya Crab House by calling or sending a WhatsApp message to +91 9382682425. State your preference (live or cleaned/cut) and quantity. Crabs are delivered to your door within 2-3 hours.

What is the price of mud crabs per kg in Kolkata?

At Baidya Crab House, live male mud crabs start at ₹349/kg, and live female mud crabs with 100% ghilu (eggs) start at ₹699/kg. Prices vary according to daily river catches from the Sundarbans.

Does Baidya Crab House provide a 100% Ghilu guarantee?

Yes! Baidya Crab House guarantees that 10 out of 10 female mud crabs are loaded with rich ghilu (coral/egg).

Do you offer free crab cleaning and cutting?

Yes, on all orders of 3 kg or more, cleaning and cutting is 100% free.

Which areas in Kolkata do you deliver to?

We deliver across Kolkata, Sonarpur, Jadavpur, Science City, Howrah, Bidhannagar, Salt Lake, New Town, Rajarhat, Dum Dum, Barasat, Barrackpore, and surrounding locations.
