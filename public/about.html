Our Journey

Bridging the Estuary & Your Table

Baidya Crab House was created with a clear, focused purpose: connecting authentic Sundarban river crab harvests directly with seafood lovers across Kolkata.

For generations, the delta of the Sundarbans has been renowned for produces the sweetest, meatiest mud crabs in South Asia. However, by the time ordinary market crabs reach municipal retail stalls, they often spend days passing through multiple wholesalers, losing moisture, sweetness, and vitality.

We eliminated the middlemen. By working closely with local river fishermen who know the tidal creeks of the Sundarbans like the back of their hands, we source freshly harvested live crabs each morning and deliver them directly to your home in Kolkata within 2 to 3 hours.

Quality Process

How We Guarantee Freshness

From the mangrove rivers to your doorstep in 4 disciplined steps

1

Daily Tide Catch

Local fishermen harvest river mud crabs during low tide in the Sundarban delta creeks, selecting active and robust crabs.

2

Rigorous Grading

Crabs are categorized by weight (from 80g up to 500g+ XXL) and female crabs are inspected for dense egg/coral maturity.

3

Cold-Pack Dispatch

Depending on your preference, crabs are either tied securely for live transport or cleaned and cut under hygienic conditions, then packed with ice.
